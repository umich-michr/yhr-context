# Study Management

An operational study posting is the participant-facing recruiting representation of an imported
institutional study.

## Posting workflow

A study posting:

- References one imported `study_num`
- Is unique for that `study_num`
- Begins inactive
- Contains participant-facing study information
- Defines participant type
- May define eligibility criteria
- May contain one screening questionnaire
- Is governed by institutional publishability
- Is activated and deactivated through study-team actions and system rules
