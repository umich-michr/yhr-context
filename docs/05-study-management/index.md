# Study Management

An operational study posting is a recruiting representation of an imported institutional study.

A posting:

- References one `study_num`
- Is unique for that `study_num`
- Has participant-facing content
- May have eligibility criteria
- May have a screening questionnaire
- Has an activation state and deactivation date
- Is controlled by institutional publishability
