## Investigation order

1. Identify the institutional deployment.
2. Identify the actor type.
3. Confirm authentication.
4. Confirm account status.
5. Confirm imported study existence.
6. Confirm the latest import and reconciliation status.
7. Confirm publishability.
8. Confirm operational study status.
9. Confirm study membership.
10. Confirm participant status and visibility.
11. Confirm eligibility and interest state.
12. Confirm field-level or export permission.
